Enter the last four results for each die. The tool estimates which chosen color is most likely to appear exactly once across all four dice.

1. Enter the last 4 rolls for each die

2. Choose the color you want to win with

Winning condition: your chosen color appears exactly once among the four dice.

3. Prediction

Enter the 16 previous results, then select Analyze & predict.

Important: This is a statistical estimate, not a guaranteed predictor. Four past results per die are a very small sample. If the dice are fair and independent, past rolls do not reveal the next roll. The tool uses smoothed frequencies so a color missing from the short history is not treated as impossible. Its confidence is not a proven accuracy rate.
